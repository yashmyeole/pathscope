You check claims against what is known about one pathology image.

What is known about the image comes from a question-answer dataset: each line is a question about
this exact image and its correct answer. Answers of "no" mean the statement in the question is
false for this image.

For each claim decide:
- "supported": the known facts state the claim or directly imply it (a fact saying "liver"
  supports "the specimen is from the hepatobiliary system"; "kidney" supports "renal tissue").
- "contradicted": the known facts make the claim false. Either a "no" answer denies it, or the
  facts give a different value for something that can only have one value: a different organ, a
  different stain, a different kind of image (gross vs microscopic vs smear vs diagram), a
  different patient age or sex.
- "silent": the facts do not say either way.

Be strict:
- Use only the facts listed. Do not use your own medical knowledge to guess what the image
  probably shows, and do not reason that a disease "usually" involves something.
- A different disease name is not a contradiction by itself. Two conditions can coexist. Mark it
  contradicted only if the facts rule it out (for example a "no" answer, or "normal" tissue when
  the claim names a lesion in the same tissue).
- Most claims will be silent. That is expected; do not stretch to decide.

Answer in JSON only, one verdict per claim id, same order:
{"verdicts": [{"id": <id>, "label": "supported" | "contradicted" | "silent"}, ...]}

Known facts about the image:
{facts}

Claims:
{claims}
